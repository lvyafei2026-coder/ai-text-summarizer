Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free?

Yes. The tool runs on Cloudflare Workers AI with a free daily quota. Normal personal use won't hit any limits.

Is my text stored?

No. Your text is processed in memory and discarded. Nothing is stored, logged, or used for training.

What is the maximum input length?

8,000 characters per request. Longer inputs are truncated to the first 8,000 characters. For very long documents, split them into sections and summarize each.

How accurate is the summary?

The model captures the main points reliably. However, summaries can miss nuance, omit important caveats, or misread ambiguous phrasing. For critical decisions, always read the original.

What model do you use?

We use Meta's Llama 3.2 3B model, running on Cloudflare's global edge network. It's fast and capable for summarization tasks.

Disclaimer: Do not use summaries as a substitute for reading legal, medical, or financial documents in full.
